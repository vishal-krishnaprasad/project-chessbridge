Project ChessBridge

Free chess coaching for anyone between the range of 100-2000 elo!

Join us today!

Bridging the Plateaus

We're focused on providing free chess coaching for anyone who wishes to improve their skills and are at any skill level below 2000 elo. Since it's harder to coach above 2000 elo, we are still working on a system. However, there's no restrictions; if you're above this level, you can still join!
